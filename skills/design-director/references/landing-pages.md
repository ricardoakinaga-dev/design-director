# Landing pages

## Narrative before modules

Define the visitor's intent and the page's one primary conversion. Build a narrative rhythm: orientation, value, proof or mechanism that actually exists, details, objections, and next step. Remove a section if the product cannot support its claims.

## Above the fold

The first viewport needs a clear proposition, useful context, and an obvious next action. A hero image should reinforce the product and audience, not occupy space because a template expects one. Keep copy width, line breaks, and CTA hierarchy intentional.

## Page-specific design system

Establish type, palette, spacing, container, button, link, media, and section rules before composing the page. Use repetition to create rhythm, then introduce one or two distinctive art-directed moments. Do not turn every section into a new card style.

## Proof and trust

Use real product capabilities, customer evidence, legal language, pricing, and logos only when supplied or verified. Label placeholders and do not invent metrics, testimonials, certifications, or partner marks.

## Responsive and conversion QA

Test long copy, keyboard focus, mobile CTA reachability, menu behavior, image crop, sticky elements, form errors, reduced motion, and slow image loading. A polished desktop wrapper with broken mobile hierarchy is not a finished landing page.
